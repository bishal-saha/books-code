# Shipping policy (v3, effective 1 March 2025)

> Kestrel Outfitters is a fictional company; this document is synthetic.

- Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day ship the same day from the Main (Riverside) warehouse; bulky items ship from North (Eastgate).
- Standard delivery promise: 3 working days. Express: 2 working days. Bulky (over 20 kg): 5 working days.
- A shipment is **late** when it is delivered after its promised date. Lost parcels count as late.
- Late deliveries on Express orders are refunded the express fee automatically.
- Carriers in use in 2025: Northline Freight, Swiftway Parcel, Harbour Express, Tallpine Logistics. Carrier performance is reviewed every quarter by Operations (Tomasz Wójcik).
