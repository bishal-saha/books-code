# Returns policy

> Kestrel Outfitters is a fictional company; this document is synthetic.

- Consumers may return unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund; trade customers within 14 days, unless their contract says otherwise.
- Damaged or wrong items: Kestrel pays return shipping and refunds within 5 working days of receiving the item.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method. Support (Maya Okafor's team) can approve refunds up to USD 500; above that, Finance approves.
